| 10026 | Playback negotiation returns 503 no_transcode_capacity when one account starts the same title on several devices at once | done | playback-verify worker | transcode-shared-10026 | 20.260 |  | Reproduced live 2026-10-11 with three concurrent GET /api/v1/playback/<file> calls from one account on three device ids: two return 200 and the third 503 no_transcode_capacity (repeatable). Root cause: each device that cannot direct-play spawned its own ffmpeg through `reserve_capacity` in `playarr-transcode` (try_acquire on a node-local semaphore, `PLAYARR_TRANSCODE_MAX_CONCURRENT_JOBS`, 2 on the regional servers), and `release_replaced_transcodes` only frees the same user, device and file. Implemented on branch `transcode-shared-10026` (not merged: needs Opus review (streaming), which is unavailable until 17 Oct, hence the ETA): admission weighting (a slot is four permits, a video-copy remux takes one, a full re-encode four); a second playback session of the same account with the same file, profile, audio stream and start position attaches to the running transcode (reference count, ffmpeg stops with the last viewer; dubs never share); 503 now carries Retry-After, and the web player (10 retries, 2 s to 10 s) and the Android player retry with back-off instead of failing. Tests: transcode crate (weights, attach and refcount), playarr-api (three devices share one transcode and stop one by one, 503 plus Retry-After), vitest and Android unit tests. Review fixes (Fable): (H1) clients stop the old session before renegotiating, so a stop event that releases a linked transcode records the viewer (user, device, file) for 30 s and the renegotiation counts as admitted; (accepted limit) the 30 s grace also admits a restart of the same title by the same device; (M1b) a borrowed slot holds no permit and never touches the semaphore, so the pool cannot grow; (M1) a renegotiation by an already admitted viewer (seek, audio or quality switch, recovery of a dropped session) is never refused for capacity: when its old link was the last one the transcode is released first and its permits reused, and when others still share the old transcode the new spawn borrows one slot over the limit (the borrowed permits leave the pool when that transcode ends, so each admitted viewer holds at most one borrowed slot at a time; viewers without a running transcode are still refused); (M2) a per-key guard held across find, spawn and register makes racing first requests spawn once; (L1) a full peer's no_transcode_capacity is forwarded as 503 with Retry-After; the Android player now also retries capacity refusals on seek, switch and session recovery. The web seek, quality and audio switches also retry a capacity refusal with the same backoff and stay on the switching state. Not done: raising the slot count (do it only after review); step 4 of the proposal. Note: 4K to 720p real-time transcoding rebuffered once (30 s) on the current node capacity during the 20.260 replay, so a full re-encode still needs a whole slot.
